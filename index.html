Offline Mandarin picture books for ages 6–10.

A dragon who tells stories in Chinese

Longlong takes children through 26 illustrated picture books — Lunar New Year lanterns, a panda's bamboo breakfast, the rabbit in the moon, the Great Wall, the Silk Road, shadow puppets, and more. Every page has Mandarin narration, pinyin, and English, so a child who speaks no Chinese can start on page one.
